# Public Bio rules (custom_label_3801446)
Format: "<headline> • <point> • <point> ..." as plain text. Split on "•". No HTML, no markdown.
1. Verify identity first: a source counts only if it matches at least 2 CRM facts (email domain, LinkedIn URL, company, city). If identity is unverified, write nothing and flag the person for Chad.
2. Never repeat header data: name, photo, location, email, phone, LinkedIn, Investor Level, ticket size, Transactor Type.
3. Headline, under 100 characters: current role, firm with a short descriptor, and tenure. No point may repeat the headline.
4. 3–6 points, one sentence each, in this order: technical or professional background; education and honors; personal interests (label the source and year if older than 3 years); public investing activity; one call-prep point last.
5. Public sources only: LinkedIn, company sites, press, SEC filings, Crunchbase, academic profiles. Never use CRM notes, Summary, deal history or private conversations, because tenants see this field.
6. Stated facts only. Never infer wealth, accreditation, net worth, age or ethnicity. Nationality only when the person states it themselves in a public source.
7. Always state whether public investing activity (angel, VC, board, fund roles) was found, including "none found".
8. The call-prep point is framed as advice to the tenant, never as a claim about the person.
9. Neutral tone, no praise adjectives. Dates as "Oct 2013" or "2009–2014".
10. If fewer than 2 points can be verified, leave the field empty and do not pad.
11. Drafts are approved by Chad before any write; nothing auto-publishes.
